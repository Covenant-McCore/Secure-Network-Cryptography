What happens when you upload

  1. Authorize — your role is verified server-side.
  2. Fingerprint — SHA-256 of the plaintext is computed for integrity reference.
  3. Key establishment — ML-KEM-768 encapsulates a shared secret to the recipient.
  4. Key derivation — HKDF-SHA-256 derives three independent keys (filename, MIME, content).
  5. Encryption — filename, MIME, and content are each sealed with AES-256-GCM.
  6. Signature — the ciphertext envelope is signed with Ed25519.
  7. Storage — only the sealed envelope plus metadata reach Firestore.

The recipient will verify the signature, decapsulate the shared secret, decrypt the three components, and confirm the SHA-256 fingerprint matches the stored value.
